## Domain Highlights

### Computer Science & AI
- **US**: NSF GRFP, NSF CAREER, DARPA YFA
- **EU**: ERC grants, Marie Curie
- **Industry**: Google/Meta/Microsoft PhD Fellowships
- **OSS**: Sovereign Tech Fund, NLnet, Prototype Fund

### Life Sciences & Biomedicine
- **US**: NIH (R01, F31, K99/R00), HHMI
- **EU**: ERC, EMBO, Wellcome Trust
- **Global**: Marie Curie, Schmidt Science Fellows

### Engineering & Physical Sciences
- **US**: NSF, DOE, Packard, Sloan
- **EU**: ERC, Alexander von Humboldt
- **UK**: EPSRC, Royal Society

### Open Tech / OSS
- **Infrastructure**: Sovereign Tech Fund, OpenSSF
- **Development**: NLnet, Mozilla, Prototype Fund
- **Ecosystem**: Python, Rust, NumFOCUS foundations

### Data & Math
20+ opportunities spanning statistics, data science, applied mathematics, and theoretical computer science. Top programs include NSF (GRFP, CAREER), the Simons Foundation (Investigator awards), and ERC grants, alongside the Sloan Fellowship and NumFOCUS Small Development Grants.

[View all Data & Math grants →](./data-math/README.md)

### Environment & Earth
18+ opportunities covering climate science, ecology, and earth and planetary science. Major funders are NSF, DOE (Early Career), ERC, and NASA (Postdoctoral Program), with the Packard Fellowship a notable option for early-career faculty.

[View all Environment & Earth grants →](./environment-earth/README.md)

### Social Sciences & Policy
19+ opportunities for economics, sociology, political science, law, and public policy. Leading programs come from NSF, NEH (Fellowships), ERC, and the British Academy (Postdoctoral Fellowships).

[View all Social Sciences & Policy grants →](./social-sciences/README.md)

### Humanities & Arts
14+ opportunities across history, philosophy, languages, arts, and digital humanities. Key funders include NEH (Fellowships), ERC, the British Academy, and the Leverhulme Trust.

[View all Humanities & Arts grants →](./humanities-arts/README.md)

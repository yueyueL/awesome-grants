## 📆 Recurring Annual Deadlines

### January

**Typical Deadlines:**
- **ERC Consolidator Grants** - European Research Council
  - Amount: Up to €2M over 5 years
  - Audience: Researchers 7-12 years post-PhD
  - Usually: Late January/Early February

### February

**Typical Deadlines:**
- **NIH K99/R00 Pathway to Independence** - NIH
  - Amount: Up to $249K per phase
  - Audience: Postdocs
  - Cycles: February, June, October
- **NSF Graduate Research Fellowship (GRFP)** - NSF (some disciplines)
  - Amount: $37,000/year + $12,000 COE
  - Audience: Graduate students
  - Note: Deadline varies by field (Oct-Feb)

### March

**Typical Deadlines:**
- **Various NSF Programs** - Domain-specific deadlines
- **Horizon Europe Calls** - European Commission (varies)

### April

**Typical Deadlines:**
- **NIH F31 Predoctoral Fellowships** - NIH
  - Amount: Stipend + tuition
  - Audience: PhD students
  - Cycles: April, August, December

### May

**Typical Deadlines:**
- **Various Foundation Grants** - Rolling or spring deadlines

### June

**Typical Deadlines:**
- **NIH K99/R00** - NIH
  - Second cycle
- **Summer Research Programs** - Various organizations

### July

**Typical Deadlines:**
- **NSF CAREER Award** - NSF
  - Amount: ~$400,000-$500,000 over 5 years
  - Audience: Early-career faculty
  - Note: Varies by directorate (July-October)

### August

**Typical Deadlines:**
- **NIH F31 Predoctoral Fellowships** - NIH
  - Third cycle
- **Some NSF Programs** - Domain-specific

### September

**Typical Deadlines:**
- **Sloan Research Fellowships** - Alfred P. Sloan Foundation
  - Amount: $75,000 over 2 years
  - Audience: Early-career scholars
  - Usually: Mid-September

### October

**Typical Deadlines:**
- **NSF GRFP** - NSF (most disciplines)
  - Amount: $37,000/year + $12,000 COE
  - Audience: Graduate students
  - Usually: Mid-October
- **NIH K99/R00** - NIH
  - Third cycle
- **Hertz Foundation Fellowship** - Hertz Foundation
  - Amount: Up to $250,000
  - Audience: PhD students (applied sciences)
  - Usually: Late October

### November

**Typical Deadlines:**
- **Various International Programs**

### December

**Typical Deadlines:**
- **NIH F31** - NIH
  - Fourth cycle (some years)
- **Year-end Foundation Grants**

## 🎯 Tips for Deadline Management

### Set Up Alerts

**For Live Grants:**
- Run the scraper monthly: `python src/master_scraper.py`
- Subscribe to [Grants.gov email alerts](https://www.grants.gov/)
- Follow funding agencies on social media

**For Curated Grants:**
- Mark recurring deadlines in your calendar
- Set reminders 6 months before
- Check agency websites quarterly

### Multi-Grant Strategy

Don't put all eggs in one basket! Successful researchers often have:
- **3-5 applications** in preparation at any time
- **Mix of deadlines** across the year
- **Different risk levels** (safe bets + reach grants)

### Resubmission Planning

If rejected:
- ✅ Read reviews immediately
- ✅ Start revisions within 1 month
- ✅ Most agencies allow resubmission
- ✅ Resubmissions have higher success rates

## 📊 Coverage

### By Region
- **🇺🇸 United States** - NSF, NIH, DOE, NASA, DARPA, foundations
- **🇪🇺 European Union** - ERC, Horizon Europe, Marie Curie
- **🇬🇧 United Kingdom** - UKRI, Wellcome Trust, Royal Society
- **🌏 Asia-Pacific** - Japan, Australia, Singapore, Hong Kong
- **🌍 Global** - Open-source funds, travel grants, microgrants

### By Type
- **Fellowships** - PhD, postdoc, and faculty fellowships
- **Research Grants** - Project funding from $10K to $2M+
- **Microgrants** - $500-$10K for small projects
- **OSS Grants** - Open-source specific funding
- **Travel Grants** - Conference and collaboration support

### By Domain
- **CS & AI** - Machine learning, systems, theory
- **Life Sciences** - Biology, medicine, health
- **Engineering** - All engineering disciplines
- **Social Sciences** - Economics, psychology, sociology
- **Humanities** - History, literature, philosophy
- **Open Tech/OSS** - Open-source software and hardware

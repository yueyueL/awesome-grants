## 🔥 Popular starting points

- [**NSF Graduate Research Fellowship (GRFP)**](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/) — flagship US PhD fellowship <span class="ag-pill">Annual · October</span>
- [**ERC Starting Grant**](https://erc.europa.eu/apply-grant/starting-grant) — up to €1.5M for early-career PIs in Europe <span class="ag-pill">Annual · October</span>
- [**NLnet Foundation Open Call**](https://nlnet.nl/propose/) — open-source and open-internet projects <span class="ag-pill">Every ~2 months</span>
- [**Google PhD Fellowship**](https://research.google/programs-and-events/phd-fellowship/) — funding plus a Google research mentor <span class="ag-pill">Annual</span>

Every entry is hand-curated, and a weekly automated refresh pulls in live opportunities so you're never chasing an expired deadline. See what just landed on the [Live Dashboard](./docs/reference/live-dashboard.md).
